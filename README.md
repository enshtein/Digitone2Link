# Digitone2Link

Digitone2Link helps Elektron Digitone II owners understand and explore the preset library stored on their instrument. Connect your Digitone II over USB MIDI to browse its preset banks and see which sound pack each preset originally came from.

Unlike Elektron Transfer, Digitone2Link displays the tags assigned to every preset, making it easier to find sounds and understand what is in your collection. The library overview shows which instrument and sound categories appear most often, so you can instantly see the balance of basses, leads, pads, drums, effects, and other types of sounds on your Digitone II.

Digitone2Link also keeps a local copy of your presets for convenient browsing and safekeeping. Your files remain on your computer, and the app does not modify sounds stored on the instrument.
